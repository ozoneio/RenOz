Mobile: tap the walking icon in the bottom row to enter Walk or return to Orbit. Drag the joystick to move and swipe the scene to look. Aim at a nearby door or window and tap Open / Close, or tap it directly. In Orbit, drag to rotate, pinch to zoom and use two fingers to pan.

Doors & windows: click any of them (in walk mode aim the crosshair and click) to open / close.

Coffee table: drag it with the mouse or a finger in Orbit mode to reposition it within the lounge.
